Preserving Anime, Manga & Animation History — Free and Independent.

A message to every fan, historian, key-animation enthusiast, and reader worldwide.

When anime and manga history is locked behind corporate walled gardens, fragmented licensing contracts, or fleeting streaming platforms, decades of artistry risk fading into obscurity. AniPedia was founded on a simple principle: every production deserves an exhaustive, permanent historical record.

We manually document every single episode recap, script outline, character transformation, original seiyū pairing, dub regionalization, storyboard artist cut, and underground doujin or 18+ OVA milestone. We believe that uncensored, open access to animation archives must belong to the public domain—not subscription tiers or algorithm-driven feeds.

AniPedia operates without third-party advertisements, tracking scripts, or commercial brand sponsors. To keep our redundant storage clusters running, maintain high-speed CDN delivery for frame archives, and safeguard our offline datasets, we rely solely on community contributions from readers who value preserved otaku culture.
