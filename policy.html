AniPedia: Policies, Guidelines and Legal Terms

Official compendium of archival standards, privacy, mature content governance, and licensing agreements.

Last edited on October 18, 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026 • Document ID: POL-2026-V1 • Permanent URL: anipedia.org/wiki/AniPedia:Policy
Binding Administrative Policy & Community Standard

This document outlines the core administrative policies and binding terms of AniPedia. Adherence is mandatory for editors, contributors, and users browsing mature and public catalog archives. Edits that contravene these guidelines are subject to immediate rollback and reviewer sanction.

§ 1.0 Archival Mission & Neutral Point of View (NPOV)

#1.0

AniPedia operates as a free, decentralized, and uncompromised encyclopedia dedicated to chronicling television anime, theatrical features, independent global animations, serialized manga, visual novels, voice artists (*seiyū* and international dub casts), and historic production studios.

All encyclopedic records must be authored from a strict Neutral Point of View (NPOV). Articles, episode logs, and production records must present historical, thematic, and biographical facts without editorial bias, commercial hype, fandom polarization, or defamatory critique. Subjective qualities (such as visual pacing or public reception) must be framed strictly within attributed secondary reporting from verified industry publications, festival acknowledgements, or authenticated sales records.

Core Operational Directive:

AniPedia rejects arbitrary canon censorship, corporate promotional prioritization, and algorithmic bias. The catalog treats a 1963 black-and-white broadcast with identical archival gravity to a modern broadcast phenomenon.

§ 2.0 Manual Data Entry & Verification Standards

#2.0

Unlike automated database scrapers, AniPedia prioritizes deliberate, human-authored archival chronologies. Contributors logging series metadata, individual episode play-by-plays, and chapter breakdowns must conform to verified attribution protocols.

  • Episode-by-Episode Synopses: Episode recaps must provide accurate, scene-accurate chronological narrative benchmarks. Fan theories, unverified leaks, and speculative future plot arcs are strictly prohibited in canonical episode bodies.
  • Air Date Chronology: Original broadcast premiere dates must follow Japanese Standard Time (JST) for domestic television releases, accompanied by regional streaming debut timestamps where dual broadcasts occurred.
  • Voice Cast Attribution: Character records must distinguish original Japanese voice actors (*seiyū*) from licensed international dub performers (English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, etc.). Reprised or alternate OVA/game voice credits must cite specific production releases.
  • Verification Hierarchy: Priority of proof follows: (1) Official broadcast end credits, (2) Publisher tankōbon releases and official studio artbooks, (3) Credited industry interviews, and (4) Reputable trade registries (such as the Japan Media Arts Database).

§ 3.0 Mature Content & 18+ (Hentai / Ecchi) Vault Regulations

#3.0

AniPedia recognizes that adult animation, erotic manga (*hentai*, *doujinshi*), and mature theatrical works hold significant subcultural and historical lineage in the broader development of modern animation techniques. Such materials are cataloged under rigorous safeguarding guidelines.

Strict Separation and Opt-In Access

All 18+ adult animated entries, erotic OVA logs, and mature visual novel adaptations are strictly sequestered behind the AniPedia 18+ Vault. Adult entries do not surface in the default Safe-For-Work index, open home carousels, or public RSS feeds without authenticated client-side opt-in confirmation.

  • Strict Statutory Compliance: AniPedia enforces a zero-tolerance policy against any visual, textual, or suggested depiction of non-consensual exploitation or underage real-world harm. All fictional portrayals must comply fully with international host jurisdictions and the Protect Our Children Act.
  • Archival Framing Only: Adult entries are cataloged exclusively through production metadata: studio circle, scenario writer, release epoch, pseudonymous voice credits, and objective synopsis. Graphic explicit previews or unrated video streams are neither hosted nor promoted.
  • Age Verification Requirement: Users accessing mature vault articles must have confirmed they are at least 18 years of age (or the legal age of majority in their applicable jurisdiction).

§ 4.0 Copyright, Fair Use & DMCA Takedown Procedures

#4.0

AniPedia is a non-profit educational and historical reference compendium. We respect the intellectual property rights of animation studios, mangaka, distributors, and creators.

Fair Use Declaration: Visual media displayed across catalog cards, character profiles, and production infoboxes—including low-resolution title key visuals, book cover art, character reference portraits, and illustrative screenshots—are reproduced under the Fair Use doctrine of the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S. Code § 107) for non-commercial educational analysis, historical chronicle, critical review, and scholarship.

DMCA Agent Notice & Designated Takedown Procedure

Copyright owners or designated agents requesting removal of media assets must transmit a formal notification pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) containing:

  1. Physical or electronic signature of the authorized copyright holder.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Exact URL(s) on AniPedia where the alleged infringing asset is located.
  4. Sufficient contact info: full name, address, telephone number, and email.
  5. A good-faith statement that use is not authorized by the copyright owner.
  6. A penalty of perjury statement affirming the notice accuracy.

Send formal notifications to: dmca@anipedia.org. Valid requests are reviewed and processed within 48 to 72 business hours.

§ 5.0 User Privacy & Local Storage Data Policy

#5.0

AniPedia is built on an unyielding zero-tracking commitment. We believe privacy is fundamental to open research and knowledge preservation.

  • No Third-Party Advertising or Tracking Pixels: AniPedia does not embed telemetry beacons, cross-site surveillance cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party ad network tags.
  • Client-Side State Storage: Preferences such as dark mode toggles, filter combinations, reading history, and mature vault confirmations are stored exclusively in your browser's client-side localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.
  • Server Access Logs: Standard HTTP server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URI) are held in ephemeral memory for temporary denial-of-service mitigation and security diagnostics, after which logs are routinely scrubbed.

§ 6.0 Creative Commons Attribution Licensing

#6.0

Unless explicitly noted otherwise, all original prose, written episode chronologies, character summaries, and compiled structural datasets authored for AniPedia are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0).

You Are Free To:

Share, copy, and redistribute text content in any medium or format, and adapt or remix material for non-commercial or commercial purposes, provided you credit AniPedia.

Under the Terms of:

Attribution (credit AniPedia with link to entry) and ShareAlike (if you alter or build upon the work, you must distribute under the identical or compatible license).

Note: Trademarked character logos, studio insignias, and copyrighted promotional artwork remain the sole property of their respective creators and are excluded from the CC BY-SA 4.0 grant.

§ 7.0 Community Conduct & Content Disclaimers

#7.0

AniPedia thrives on open collaboration. Contributors, reviewers, and patrons are expected to interact with mutual scholarly respect.

  • Civility & Anti-Harassment: Ad-hominem attacks, doxxing, brigading against voice actors or studio teams, and personal disputes on discussion pages are subject to immediate editorial suspension.
  • Vandalism & Hoaxes: Falsification of broadcast dates, insertion of manufactured quotes, vandalism of character sexual orientation or voice credits, and fictitious show creation will result in a permanent ban.
  • Spoiler Policy: As an exhaustive encyclopedic archive, AniPedia documents narrative works in full, including climaxes, character fates, and series endings. Readers browse with the understanding that unmasked spoilers exist throughout article bodies and episode recaps.
  • General Disclaimer of Accuracy: While voluntary curators work diligently to verify all records against primary materials, AniPedia provides its encyclopedia on an "as-is" basis without warranties of completeness or fitness for particular commercial applications.

§ 8.0 Amendments, Dispute Resolution & Contact Details

#8.0

The AniPedia Preservation Council reserves the right to periodically revise these administrative policies to address evolving digital copyright legislation, community consensus, and archival scope. Material updates are posted to this register with an updated version hash and 14-day notice.
