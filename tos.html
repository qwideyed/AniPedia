AniPedia is organized as a free, open, and non-commercial public interest encyclopedia. Our sole mandate is the historical preservation and structured indexing of anime television series, theatrical films, global and independent animations, serialized manga, visual novels, voice artists (*seiyū* and localized dub performers), character genealogies, and animation production studios.

Permanent Open Access Guarantee:

AniPedia will never implement a paywall, subscription barrier, or gated premium tier for reading encyclopedic records, episode chronologies, or character metadata. All core historical reference materials remain free for the global public.

§ 3.0 User Conduct, Manual Submissions & Curation Standards

#3.0

Because AniPedia prioritizes meticulous human-authored data entry over automated web scraping, every contributor assumes direct accountability for the factual integrity and legal compliance of their written submissions.

  • Attribution and Accuracy: Episode-by-episode breakdowns, plot synopses, and voice cast credits must be authored accurately based on authenticated primary broadcast credits, official studio artbooks, tankōbon volumes, or certified industry archives.
  • Strict Prohibition on Vandalism: Intentionally falsifying release dates, inventing fictitious production houses, inserting defamatory statements against living voice artists or staff, and defacing character profiles will result in immediate IP termination.
  • Absolute Zero-Tolerance Content: Any attempt to submit, link, upload, or describe real-world non-consensual sexual abuse, unlawful child exploitation imagery (CSAM/CSAE), terroristic threat material, or doxxing dossiers is strictly prohibited and will be reported to international law enforcement and NCMEC without notice.
  • Automated Flooding: Contributors must not use bot scrapers or automated query engines to spam our manual submission queues or overwhelm client-side state engines.

Client-Side Isolation

Adult content is strictly sequestered within the 18+ Vault and will never appear in open Safe-For-Work feeds or portal directories unless the user has opted in via local client-side confirmation.

  • Archival Scope Only: Mature entries are documented solely via factual metadata: creator circles, release epochs, pseudonymous voice actor pairings, and objective narrative framing. Unrated or explicit video streams are strictly outside the scope of our repository.
  • Jurisdictional Compliance: You are responsible for ensuring that viewing adult animation metadata and historical summaries complies with the statutory requirements of your local municipal, provincial, or national jurisdiction.
